Mental Health & Wellness Companion Project Overview

Users: Individuals (young adults, students, and general users) looking for accessible, basic, and non-clinical mental health support and wellness tips.

Core Value: The site provides immediate, simple, and private (no login) educational content and AI-generated supportive messages to help users cope with daily stress and improve mental well-being.

Key Wellness Topics:
1.  Breathing Exercises: Guided techniques for immediate stress relief.
2.  Healthy Habits: Information on the link between sleep, nutrition, and mental health.
3.  Stress Reduction Techniques: Tips like mindfulness and progressive muscle relaxation.
4.  Daily Positivity: A section for affirmations and uplifting quotes.
